My Impact

My first task at Smartist: in one week I replaced manual cropping with AI background removal in the add-artwork flow. Adding an artwork became 22% faster, round and free-form works now make up 9% of all artworks — bringing new artists to the app — and as part of Pro the feature lifted conversion to subscription from this flow by 6%.

Context

Smartist lets artists photograph a painting and see how it looks on a wall. To add an artwork, people tapped plus, chose a photo, cropped the artwork by hand, adjusted the colours — and only then saw it in the app. Manual cropping often came out crooked, and it didn’t work at all for paintings that aren’t rectangles.

Challenge

Make adding an artwork simpler and the result cleaner — for paintings of any shape — and raise LTV with new paid features. All within one week, as my first task in the company.

Behind the scenes

One week, first task

This was my first task at Smartist, and I had one week for it. I started with apps that already removed backgrounds automatically — mostly photo editors. I collected examples of how the feature works and looks there, different animations for the moment the background disappears, and how people remove a background by hand when they need to.

Why it became a Pro feature

Every removal was a request to our server, and each one cost us money. So instead of giving it away for free, we made it part of the Pro subscription, next to the other perks artists already paid for.

AI removes the background in one tap

Pick a photo, and AI finds the painting and clears the wall and everything around it.

Touch up when AI misses a spot

If a bit of background stays or part of the painting gets cut, tap Touch up. Erase leftovers or restore lost parts with a brush, change the brush size, and use the loupe to see the edge under your finger.

Artworks of any shape

Before, the app could only crop a photo to a rectangle, so round and free-form works couldn’t be added. Now an artwork keeps its real shape and looks right on the wall in the interior.

Outcome

We compared analytics before and after launch, and after each removal asked people to rate the result — to collect feedback on how well the AI worked. The flow got faster, the app opened up to new artists, and Pro got one more reason to subscribe. LTV grew as well.
