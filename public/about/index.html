I'm Viktoriia Polotniuk

Kyiv, Ukraine

I’m a product designer with 4+ years of experience. I found design while looking for what I really wanted to do. A course at Mate Academy turned into a love for design and everything around it — and soon I was mentoring new students there myself.

Since then I’ve gone from a design agency to being the only designer at Smartist, and then to leading the app redesign at Nebula. Along the way I learned to own a product end to end: start with a hypothesis, design to test it, and let the data decide what ships.

Open to new opportunities. Reach out if you wanna be friends or colleagues!
